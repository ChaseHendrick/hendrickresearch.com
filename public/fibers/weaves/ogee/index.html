Motif

Ogee

Opposing S-shaped curves meet to form an undulating pointed repeat.

Also searched as: ogee lattice

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Mirrored curves create arch-like or stretched diamond shapes. The outline can be printed or built into a textured weave.

How to recognize it

Look for a curve that bends first one way and then the other.

Common uses

  • Upholstery
  • Cushions
  • Drapery

What to distinguish

Ogee names the curve geometry. It may form a trellis, but many trellises use straight lines or other shapes.
