Bobbin lace

Bobbin lace creates openwork by crossing and twisting multiple threads.

Also searched as: Pillow lace

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Threads wound on bobbins are worked around a pattern. Pins on a supporting pillow hold the developing network in place.

How to recognize it

Close inspection reveals interlaced thread paths forming the solid and open areas.

Common uses

  • Collars
  • Decorative edgings
  • Lace panels

What to distinguish

Needle lace builds its structure with a needle and thread; many machine laces imitate either appearance.
