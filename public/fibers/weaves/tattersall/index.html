Tattersall

A light-ground check made with alternating fine colored lines.

Also searched as: tattersall check

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Two contrasting sets of narrow stripes cross in warp and weft. Makers produce the pattern on different grounds, including plain-woven shirting.

How to recognize it

Look for open pale squares bounded by a repeating sequence of differently colored lines.

Common uses

  • Shirts
  • Waistcoats
  • Jacket linings

What to distinguish

Tattersall is more specific than a generic grid but does not require one fiber or weave. Gingham generally uses broad equal bands rather than these fine outlines.
