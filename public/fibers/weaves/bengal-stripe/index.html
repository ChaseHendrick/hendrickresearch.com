Bengal stripe

Alternating light and colored stripes of approximately equal width.

Also searched as: Regency stripe

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

In woven shirting, repeated warp color bands create the design. Thomas Pink describes its Bengal stripe as quarter-inch alternations, an example rather than a universal rule.

How to recognize it

The light and dark bands have similar visual weight.

Common uses

  • Dress shirts
  • Blouses
  • Summer clothing

What to distinguish

Bengal is generally bolder than fine shirting stripes and narrower than an awning stripe, but names and widths vary across manufacturers.
