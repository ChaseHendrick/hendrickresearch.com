3/1 twill

A four-thread twill puts three warp crossings on the face for every one below.

Also searched as: Four-end warp-faced twill

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Each warp passes over three successive picks and under one. The long warp floats create a face that can emphasize a colored warp over a lighter weft.

How to recognize it

Look for a strong diagonal and a face that differs markedly from the reverse when warp and weft colors contrast.

Common uses

  • Denim
  • Chinos
  • Workwear

What to distinguish

It shares a four-thread repeat with 2/2 twill but has unequal face and back interlacing counts.
