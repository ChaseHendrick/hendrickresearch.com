Motif

Floral

Flowers organize a design ranging from scattered sprigs to dense trailing repeats.

Also searched as: flower pattern, floral print

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

The flowers may be naturalistic or highly stylized. Printing, weaving and embroidery can each carry the motif.

How to recognize it

Look for petals, flower heads and stems, then note their spacing and direction.

Common uses

  • Dresses
  • Curtains
  • Upholstery

What to distinguish

Floral is a subject category rather than a fiber, finish or repeat size. A floral pattern is not automatically glazed chintz.
