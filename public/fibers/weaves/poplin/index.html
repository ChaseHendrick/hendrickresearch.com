Woven fabric

Poplin

A closely woven plain-weave fabric offers a smooth shirting surface, sometimes with a fine crosswise rib.

Also searched as: Popeline, Broadcloth shirting

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Single warp and weft yarns alternate in a plain weave. Relative yarn size and spacing can create a subtle rib across the cloth.

How to recognize it

Look for a fine even surface without twill diagonals or grouped basket crossings.

Common uses

  • Dress shirts
  • Blouses
  • Shirtdresses

What to distinguish

Broadcloth can mean poplin-like cotton shirting in some markets, but the term also has other textile meanings.
