Motif

Arabesque

Interlacing curved stems and stylized plant forms create an ornamental network.

Also searched as: scrolling foliage

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Textile examples combine tendrils, leaves and palmettes in repeated arrangements. The Met records printed cotton examples that echo the ornament of woven luxury textiles.

How to recognize it

Follow the connected curving stems through the repeat.

Common uses

  • Decorative textiles
  • Dress fabrics
  • Furnishings

What to distinguish

Arabesque is an ornamental vocabulary rather than one exact repeat or weave. Use a specific object’s record for its cultural attribution.
