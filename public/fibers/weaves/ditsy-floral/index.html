Motif

Ditsy floral

Small, closely repeated flowers make an all-over pattern.

Also searched as: ditzy floral, small floral, ditsie

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Tiny blossoms or sprigs are distributed across the ground. The effect can be produced as a print on many different cloths.

How to recognize it

Individual flowers remain small while the overall surface reads as a fine texture.

Common uses

  • Blouses
  • Dresses
  • Quilting

What to distinguish

Ditsy describes small, scattered patterning and is not exclusive to flowers or any one brand. Liberty’s Small Betsy is one specific floral example.
