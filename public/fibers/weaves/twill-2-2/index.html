2/2 twill

A balanced four-thread twill shows equal warp and weft interlacing counts.

Also searched as: Four-end balanced twill

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Each warp floats over two picks and under two. The crossings shift by one position on each pick to form the diagonal.

How to recognize it

Both sides have the same interlacing balance, although yarn color, density and finishing may make them look different.

Common uses

  • Suiting
  • Scarves
  • Blankets

What to distinguish

Balanced interlacing does not guarantee identical yarn sizes or equal thread density.
