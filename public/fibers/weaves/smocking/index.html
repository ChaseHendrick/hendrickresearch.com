Smocking

Smocking gathers cloth into small folds held and decorated by stitches.

Also searched as: Smocked fabric, Honeycomb smocking

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

A wide area of fabric is gathered into regular pleats. Stitches connect selected folds to control fullness and make a textured pattern.

How to recognize it

Look for stitch-linked pleats forming compact geometric areas beside looser cloth.

Common uses

  • Dress bodices
  • Sleeve cuffs
  • Decorative garment panels

What to distinguish

Honeycomb smocking describes one arrangement; a woven honeycomb texture does not use this gathering technique.
