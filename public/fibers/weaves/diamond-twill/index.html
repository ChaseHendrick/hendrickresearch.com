Diamond twill

Twill directions reverse along both axes to outline woven diamonds.

Also searched as: Lozenge twill

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

A pointed sequence across the warp is combined with a reversing sequence of picks. The resulting repeat turns the diagonal lines into lozenges rather than an uninterrupted diagonal.

How to recognize it

Trace the diagonal ribs to see whether they meet around a repeating diamond.

Common uses

  • Scarves
  • Blankets
  • Decorative cloth

What to distinguish

A diamond motif can be printed or knitted too; diamond twill specifically describes a woven interlacing.
