Tapestry weaving

Discontinuous colored wefts build a design while covering the warp.

Also searched as: Weft-faced tapestry

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Weft yarns work back and forth within selected areas rather than necessarily spanning the full width. Packing them closely hides the warp and creates blocks of color.

How to recognize it

Look for a weft-dominated face and color areas built into the fabric itself.

Common uses

  • Wall hangings
  • Cushions
  • Decorative clothing

What to distinguish

Needlepoint stitches are worked into an existing ground; tapestry weaving creates the cloth as it creates the image.
