Zebra motif

Irregular branching stripes evoke the contrasting bands on a zebra’s coat.

Also searched as: zebra print, zebra stripe

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Printed and woven versions vary the band width, direction and color. Designers often stylize the marks rather than reproduce a particular animal.

How to recognize it

Look for tapered, curving bands that fork or end unevenly.

Common uses

  • Upholstery
  • Fashion prints
  • Accessories

What to distinguish

Zebra is an animal-inspired motif, not a fiber or hide claim. A simple even stripe lacks its characteristic irregular branching.
