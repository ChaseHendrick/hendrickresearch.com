Fair Isle knitting

Fair Isle is a Shetland colorwork tradition using stranded yarns and repeating knitted motifs.

Also searched as: Fair Isle colorwork, Fair Isle colourwork

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Typically two colors are worked within each row or round. The unused yarn travels behind the stitches as short floats, while colors can change between bands.

How to recognize it

Look for repeating bands of small motifs and colored floats on the reverse.

Common uses

  • Sweaters
  • Hats
  • Gloves

What to distinguish

Not every stranded multicolor knit is Fair Isle; the name also carries a specific regional design tradition.
