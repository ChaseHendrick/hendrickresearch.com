Embossed texture

Embossing presses a relief pattern into an existing fabric surface.

Also searched as: Embossing, Embossed finish

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Engraved rollers apply pressure and sometimes heat. How well the effect lasts depends on the fibers and the setting or finishing treatment.

How to recognize it

Raised and recessed areas repeat across an otherwise continuous base fabric.

Common uses

  • Decorative apparel
  • Upholstery
  • Textured accessories

What to distinguish

An embossed texture can imitate a woven effect without using that weave; inspect the underlying yarn arrangement.
