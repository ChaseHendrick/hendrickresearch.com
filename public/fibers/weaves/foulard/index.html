Foulard motif

Small, orderly motifs repeat in a regular spaced layout.

Also searched as: neat print, foulard pattern

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Classic designs arrange compact geometric figures in rows or offset rows. The name also refers to a lightweight silk cloth and, in French, a scarf.

How to recognize it

Look for tiny individual shapes separated by a consistent ground.

Common uses

  • Neckties
  • Scarves
  • Dress prints

What to distinguish

Foulard is not one fixed symbol or one printing method. A dot repeat is only one possible member of this broader family.
