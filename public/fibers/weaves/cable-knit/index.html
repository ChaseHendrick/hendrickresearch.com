Knit structure

Cable knit

Cable knitting crosses groups of stitches to make raised ropes, braids or traveling lines.

Also searched as: Cabled knitting

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Some stitches are held in front or behind while neighboring stitches are worked. Working the held stitches afterward changes their order and creates a crossing.

How to recognize it

Follow the raised stitch columns through their repeated crossings.

Common uses

  • Sweaters
  • Hats
  • Blankets

What to distinguish

An Aran-style sweater may combine cables with several other textures; cable knit does not describe the whole tradition.
