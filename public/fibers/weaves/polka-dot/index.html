Weaves & patterns
Motif

Polka dot

Regularly repeated round spots contrast with a surrounding ground.

Also searched as: polka dots, spot pattern

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Dots can be printed, woven or embroidered. Their scale, spacing and arrangement determine how dense the pattern appears.

How to recognize it

Look for clearly round, repeated units rather than irregular animal-like spots.

Common uses

  • Dresses
  • Neckties
  • Scarves

What to distinguish

Polka dot identifies the shape, not a particular weave. Small pin dots and textured dotted Swiss deserve closer inspection because their construction may differ.
