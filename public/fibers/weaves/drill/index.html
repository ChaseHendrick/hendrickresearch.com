Drill

A firm twill fabric is widely used where clothing needs substantial body.

Also searched as: Drill cloth

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Drill commonly uses a compact 2/1 twill in cotton or manufactured fibers. The illustrated repeat shows that interlacing rather than a particular yarn weight.

How to recognize it

Look for clear diagonal ribs in a sturdy cloth.

Common uses

  • Uniforms
  • Work trousers
  • Overalls

What to distinguish

Drill is a fabric name, not a promise of a specific fiber or measured strength.
