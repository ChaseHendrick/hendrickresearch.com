Crochet

Crochet forms fabric by drawing yarn through loops with a hook.

Also searched as: Hand crochet

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Chains establish a starting structure and stitches build into earlier stitches or spaces. Different stitch heights and placements create dense cloth or open motifs.

How to recognize it

Trace the chains and stitch posts; crochet may be worked in rows, rounds or joined motifs.

Common uses

  • Garments
  • Blankets
  • Bags

What to distinguish

Hand crochet differs from knitting; industrial machinery also uses the term crochet for some warp-knitted structures.
