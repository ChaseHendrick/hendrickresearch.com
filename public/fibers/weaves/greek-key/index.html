Motif

Greek key

A continuous angular line turns repeatedly at right angles.

Also searched as: meander, fret pattern

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

The meandering line often forms a border. Textile makers can print it, weave it or embroider it onto a separate ground.

How to recognize it

Follow squared turns and repeated inward folds rather than curved loops.

Common uses

  • Borders
  • Cushions
  • Curtain trims

What to distinguish

Greek key identifies an ornamental line pattern. Schumacher’s embroidered border illustrates one method, not a required construction.
