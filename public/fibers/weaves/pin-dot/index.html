Weaves & patterns
Motif

Pin dot

Tiny repeated dots create a subtle spotted surface.

Also searched as: pindot, micro dot

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

The dots may be woven into the ground or applied as a print. Eton’s woven pin-dot shirting is one example of the structural version.

How to recognize it

The pattern can look nearly solid at a distance and separate into points close up.

Common uses

  • Shirts
  • Ties
  • Suiting

What to distinguish

Pin dot indicates small scale rather than a standardized dot diameter. Do not infer a raised surface from the name alone.
