Awning stripe

Broad, regular bands create a bold alternating stripe.

Also searched as: deckchair stripe

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Wide areas of contrasting color repeat across the surface. The design appears in both shirting and furnishing textiles.

How to recognize it

Look for bands wide enough to remain distinct from a distance.

Common uses

  • Shirts
  • Cushions
  • Awning covers

What to distinguish

The name describes the scale of the pattern. It does not mean a dress fabric has the weather resistance or strength needed for an actual awning.
