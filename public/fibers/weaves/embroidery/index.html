Embroidery

Embroidery creates decoration through stitches worked on or through a supporting material.

Also searched as: Surface embroidery, Counted-thread embroidery

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Threads may follow counted positions in the ground or move freely over a drawn design. Additional techniques attach laid threads or build raised details.

How to recognize it

Look for decorative stitches that can be distinguished from the ground fabric's own yarn structure.

Common uses

  • Garment decoration
  • Household linen
  • Decorative panels

What to distinguish

This is a broad family of techniques; similar motifs can also be printed or woven.
