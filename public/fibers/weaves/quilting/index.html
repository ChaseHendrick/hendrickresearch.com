Quilting

Quilting joins fabric layers with lines of stitching that can also form a surface design.

Also searched as: Quilted fabric

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Stitches pass through the layers, often including padding between a face and backing. Their layout controls how the layers are held and where raised areas remain.

How to recognize it

Follow the stitched channels separating the fuller areas between them.

Common uses

  • Bed covers
  • Padded jackets
  • Cushions

What to distinguish

Patchwork joins pieces into a surface; quilting joins layers, and a quilt need not contain patchwork.
