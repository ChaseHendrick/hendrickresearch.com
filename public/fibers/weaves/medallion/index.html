Motif

Medallion

A self-contained ornamental unit repeats as a rounded, oval or shaped centerpiece.

Also searched as: medallion repeat

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Designers build the unit from smaller lines, petals, dots or other elements. It may stand alone or repeat with open space around each figure.

How to recognize it

Look for an enclosed central focus inside each repeated motif.

Common uses

  • Cushions
  • Curtains
  • Upholstery

What to distinguish

Medallion describes the motif’s organization, not damask construction. A printed medallion can resemble one woven into the cloth.
