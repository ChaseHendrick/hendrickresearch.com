Gingham

An even check formed by overlapping equal-width light and colored bands.

Also searched as: Vichy check

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Traditional gingham is plain-woven from colored yarns with matching band widths in warp and weft. Crossings make pale, dark and mixed-color squares.

How to recognize it

The check repeats regularly in both directions, with intermediate tones at unlike-color intersections.

Common uses

  • Shirts
  • Dresses
  • Table linens

What to distinguish

Gingham names both a familiar check and a cloth tradition; a printed gingham look need not be yarn-dyed. It lacks houndstooth’s pointed outline.
