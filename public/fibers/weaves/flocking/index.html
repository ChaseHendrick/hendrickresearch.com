Surface & finish

Flocked surface

Flocking attaches short fibers to a surface to create a soft, raised design.

Also searched as: Flocking, Flock printing

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Adhesive is applied in the desired pattern. Short cut fibers are deposited into it, then the adhesive is fixed to hold them in place.

How to recognize it

A pile-like motif sits on top of a distinct base fabric.

Common uses

  • Decorative apparel
  • Lettered textiles
  • Furnishing fabrics

What to distinguish

Flock is added to a substrate; woven or knitted pile is incorporated while the fabric is formed.
