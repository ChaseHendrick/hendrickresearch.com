Dotted Swiss

A light cloth decorated with small spaced dots, often visibly raised.

Also searched as: Swiss dot, Swiss dots

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Traditional examples use fine plain-woven cotton with additional woven dots. Later versions use embroidery, flocking or printing, so inspect the reverse and the dot surface.

How to recognize it

A light ground separates small isolated spots; textured dots may stand above it.

Common uses

  • Blouses
  • Dresses
  • Curtains

What to distinguish

A flat polka-dot print is not the same construction as a woven clip-spot or embroidered dotted Swiss. The name covers several manufacturing routes.
