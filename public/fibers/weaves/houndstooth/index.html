Houndstooth

A broken check whose pointed corners come from color order interacting with twill.

Also searched as: dogtooth, dogstooth, pied-de-poule, puppytooth

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Classic woven houndstooth combines a 2/2 twill with four light then four dark yarns in both warp and weft. Printed versions reproduce the outline without that interlacement.

How to recognize it

Look for interlocking jagged checks rather than continuous zigzag bands.

Common uses

  • Tailored jackets
  • Coats
  • Skirts

What to distinguish

Puppytooth is a small-scale version; herringbone describes a reversal of twill direction. A houndstooth image alone does not establish the fiber or construction.
