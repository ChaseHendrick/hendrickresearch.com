Woven fabric

Brocade

Additional pattern yarns create figured decoration over a woven ground.

Also searched as: Brocaded fabric

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Supplementary yarns form decorative areas while the underlying fabric supplies its ground structure. These patterning yarns can differ in color or material, including metallic threads.

How to recognize it

Look for figured areas that appear raised or distinct from the background and inspect the extra yarns on the reverse.

Common uses

  • Formalwear
  • Draperies
  • Upholstery

What to distinguish

Brocade is a woven fabric family, not simply any floral pattern or any fabric made on a Jacquard loom.
