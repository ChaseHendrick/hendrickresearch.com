Knit structure

Ponte di Roma

Ponte is a firm double knit with a subtle horizontal rib.

Also searched as: Ponte, Ponte Roma

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Its repeat combines interlock knitting with courses worked on each needle set separately. This produces a compact fabric with similar faces.

How to recognize it

Look for fine crosswise lines on a smooth, substantial knit.

Common uses

  • Trousers
  • Jackets
  • Structured dresses

What to distinguish

Ponte is a knit, even when tailored like woven cloth.
