Motif

Chevron

Continuous angled bands meet in repeated V-shaped points.

Also searched as: zigzag stripe

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

The visible zigzag can be printed, knitted or woven. Its geometry describes the design independently of the fabrication method.

How to recognize it

Follow an unbroken band through successive peaks and valleys.

Common uses

  • Knitwear
  • Cushions
  • Drapery

What to distinguish

A woven herringbone typically has a break at each twill reversal. Chevron is the broader zigzag motif and does not by itself establish that structure.
