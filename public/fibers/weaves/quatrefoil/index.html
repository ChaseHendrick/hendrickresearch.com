Motif

Quatrefoil

Four rounded lobes surround a center to make a clover-like outline.

Also searched as: four-lobed motif

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

The motif can repeat separately or connect into a lattice. Its outline, rather than a specific textile process, defines the design.

How to recognize it

Count four lobes around each unit; pointed additions occur in some variants.

Common uses

  • Cushions
  • Window treatments
  • Decorative fabrics

What to distinguish

Quatrefoil names a four-lobed figure, while trellis names a network layout. A fabric can exhibit both.
