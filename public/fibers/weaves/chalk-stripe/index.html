Chalk stripe

A suiting stripe with a broader, softer-edged appearance than a typical pinstripe.

Also searched as: chalkstripe

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Contrasting yarns form repeated lines through the cloth. A soft finish can make their edges resemble drawn chalk.

How to recognize it

Look for a pale line with visual width and some softness against the ground.

Common uses

  • Suits
  • Jackets
  • Trousers

What to distinguish

Names overlap between suppliers, so compare stripe width, spacing and edge definition. A chalk stripe is not proof that the fabric is flannel.
