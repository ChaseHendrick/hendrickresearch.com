Color pattern

Pinstripe

Fine parallel lines repeat across a contrasting ground.

Also searched as: pin stripe

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

A narrow group of contrasting warp yarns can produce the stripes in woven suiting. The same visual layout can also be printed.

How to recognize it

The stripe is much narrower than the space between stripes.

Common uses

  • Suits
  • Trousers
  • Shirts

What to distinguish

Pinstripe describes a visible color pattern. Chalk stripes usually look wider and softer, while a twill diagonal is part of the underlying weave.
