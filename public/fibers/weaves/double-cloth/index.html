Weave structure

Double cloth

Two layers are woven together and may be joined or exchanged to form a design.

Also searched as: Double weave, Doubleweave

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Each layer has its own warp and weft system in a typical double-cloth construction. Layers can exchange positions at selected places, making reversible patterns or pockets between them.

How to recognize it

Examine both faces and an unfinished edge for two connected layers.

Common uses

  • Coverlets
  • Reversible cloth
  • Coating

What to distinguish

Double cloth is made during weaving; two separate fabrics glued or sewn together are not automatically double cloth.
