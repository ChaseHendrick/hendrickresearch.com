Motif

Botanical

Plants, leaves, branches and other botanical forms provide the subject of a repeat.

Also searched as: foliage pattern, leaf print

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Designs may closely study specimens or stylize their outlines. Printed fabric can imitate drawn or woven effects without sharing those manufacturing methods.

How to recognize it

Look for leaf shapes, branching stems, fruit or identifiable plant forms.

Common uses

  • Shirts
  • Curtains
  • Cushions

What to distinguish

Botanical overlaps floral but can emphasize foliage without blossoms. The depicted plant does not identify the textile fiber.
