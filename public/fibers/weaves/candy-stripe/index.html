Candy stripe

A fine repeated stripe usually made with light and colored bands.

Also searched as: candystripe

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Shirting makers use the term for close stripes and multicolor variations. Yarn color placement produces woven examples; the name alone does not identify the weave.

How to recognize it

The repeated light and colored lines tend to read as an even, lively rhythm.

Common uses

  • Shirts
  • Summer dresses
  • Pajamas

What to distinguish

Candy and Bengal are overlapping trade terms with no universal size boundary. Check a swatch or stated stripe width instead of assuming one fixed dimension.
