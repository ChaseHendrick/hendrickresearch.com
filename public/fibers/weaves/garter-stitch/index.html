Knit structure

Garter stitch

Garter stitch makes a reversible knit with pronounced horizontal ridges.

Also searched as: Garter knit

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

When working flat, knitting every row forms garter stitch. The fabric alternates how the rows face the viewer, rather than presenting only knit faces on one side.

How to recognize it

Look for stacked horizontal ridges with a similar appearance on both surfaces.

Common uses

  • Scarves
  • Blankets
  • Knit borders

What to distinguish

Knitting every round in circular work produces stockinette, so flat-row instructions cannot be transferred unchanged.
