Regimental stripe

Diagonal bands are arranged in repeated color groups, especially on neckties.

Also searched as: repp stripe, rep stripe

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

A tie’s cut and construction present the stripes diagonally when worn. Color order and direction can be significant in specific institutional designs.

How to recognize it

Look for a consistent diagonal sequence rather than a woven twill rib alone.

Common uses

  • Neckties
  • Bow ties
  • Scarves

What to distinguish

Repp describes a ribbed fabric and regimental describes a design tradition; neither is a universal synonym for every striped tie. Commercial stripes need not indicate an affiliation.
