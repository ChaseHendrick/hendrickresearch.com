Motif

Trellis

A connected lattice divides the surface into repeated open cells.

Also searched as: lattice pattern, treillage

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Crossing or linked lines establish the grid. Leaves, flowers or small motifs may sit within or over that framework.

How to recognize it

Look for a continuous network with open spaces between its lines.

Common uses

  • Curtains
  • Cushions
  • Upholstery

What to distinguish

Trellis describes a layout rather than one cell shape. A design may be both botanical and trellis, or both ogee and trellis.
