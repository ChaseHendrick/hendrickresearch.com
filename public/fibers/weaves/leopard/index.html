Leopard motif

Irregular rosette-like spots evoke a leopard’s coat.

Also searched as: leopard print, leopard spot

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Textile designers repeat and stylize the markings in varied palettes. Printed cloth and woven textured versions can carry the same visual idea.

How to recognize it

Look for uneven clustered rings or rosettes rather than evenly spaced circles.

Common uses

  • Dress prints
  • Upholstery
  • Accessories

What to distinguish

Leopard describes the image and does not imply animal-derived material. Cheetah-inspired motifs more often use solid spots, though commercial names can be loose.
