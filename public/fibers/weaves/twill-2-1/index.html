2/1 twill

A three-thread twill repeat places more warp than weft on the illustrated face.

Also searched as: Three-end twill

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Each warp goes over two picks and under one. Shifting the crossing by one yarn on successive picks produces a three-end, three-pick diagonal repeat.

How to recognize it

The visible floats are shorter than those in 3/1 twill, with a contrasting reverse.

Common uses

  • Light trouser fabrics
  • Uniforms
  • Shirting

What to distinguish

The numbers describe warp-over and warp-under counts, not yarn colors or the number of plies.
