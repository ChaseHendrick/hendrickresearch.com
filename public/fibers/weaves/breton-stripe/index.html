Breton stripe

Horizontal stripes associated with the French marinière and nautical knitwear.

Also searched as: marinière stripe, sailor stripe

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Classic marinières use contrasting stripes knitted into the garment cloth. Modern versions vary the palette, spacing and position of the bands.

How to recognize it

Look for horizontal color bands, often with a larger unstriped area near the neckline.

Common uses

  • Jersey tops
  • Sweaters
  • Children’s clothing

What to distinguish

Historic uniform proportions describe a particular garment standard, not every contemporary Breton top. Horizontal stripes are a pattern choice, not a weave.
