Madras check

A colorful checked cotton cloth tradition associated with Chennai, formerly Madras.

Also searched as: madras plaid, bleeding madras, patchwork madras

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Colored yarn bands create checks with varied widths and color combinations. Patchwork madras joins pieces of checked cloth, while bleeding madras identifies a dye behavior rather than every modern example.

How to recognize it

Look for lively multicolor crossings and an airy shirting appearance; patchwork versions have actual seams.

Common uses

  • Summer shirts
  • Shorts
  • Casual jackets

What to distinguish

A madras-looking print does not establish Indian manufacture or handweaving. Not every madras cloth is designed to release dye.
