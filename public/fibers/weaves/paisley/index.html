Motif

Paisley

Curved teardrop forms with bent tips build an ornamental repeat.

Also searched as: boteh, buta

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

The motif appears in woven shawls, embroidery and printed textiles. Interiors may contain smaller flowers, leaves or geometric details.

How to recognize it

Look for the hooked tip rather than a simple straight-pointed droplet.

Common uses

  • Shawls
  • Neckties
  • Dress prints

What to distinguish

The English name reflects the Scottish manufacturing center, not a claim that the motif originated there. Kashmir and Persian textile histories are central to its development.
