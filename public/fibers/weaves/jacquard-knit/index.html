Jacquard knit

Jacquard knitting uses selected stitches and yarns to build pattern into a knit.

Also searched as: Knitted jacquard

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Machine needle selection controls where colors or textures appear. Single-knit and double-knit versions manage the unused yarns differently.

How to recognize it

Inspect the reverse as well as the face: the design is made in the stitches, not simply printed over them.

Common uses

  • Patterned sweaters
  • Knit dresses
  • Decorative blankets

What to distinguish

Jacquard is a patterning method, not one motif or fiber; knitted and woven jacquards are structurally different.
