Motif

Harlequin

A repeating field of elongated diamonds arranged edge to edge.

Also searched as: harlequin diamond

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Contrasting fills emphasize the diamond grid. The layout can be printed or constructed with colored textile elements.

How to recognize it

The units are tall lozenges rather than upright squares.

Common uses

  • Costume
  • Decorative cushions
  • Fashion prints

What to distinguish

Argyle adds a fine crossing-line layer; a simple harlequin repeat need not include one. The motif does not specify fabric weight or weave.
