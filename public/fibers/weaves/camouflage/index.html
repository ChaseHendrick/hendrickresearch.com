Motif

Camouflage

Contrasting patches, marks or pixels disrupt a recognizable outline.

Also searched as: camo, disruptive pattern, digital camouflage

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Textile patterns arrange color areas at different scales. Their effect depends on the environment and viewing conditions as well as the design.

How to recognize it

Look for irregular interlocking areas or pixel groups rather than a simple regular spot repeat.

Common uses

  • Uniforms
  • Outdoor clothing
  • Fashion prints

What to distinguish

A fashion camouflage print does not establish tested concealment or suitability for a particular terrain. Digital describes one visual approach, not every camouflage.
