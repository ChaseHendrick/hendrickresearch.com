Ticking stripe

A repeated narrow stripe associated with traditional mattress ticking.

Also searched as: mattress stripe

Open the interactive guide ↗

How it is made

Typical versions group darker lines against a pale ground. Contemporary decorative textiles use the stripe on varied fabrics.

How to recognize it

Look for restrained parallel bands, often with finer companion lines.

Common uses

  • Cushions
  • Curtains
  • Bedding

What to distinguish

A ticking-style stripe is not evidence of feather-proof construction. The visual pattern and a tightly woven mattress casing are separate properties.
