Brunello Cucinelli

Brunello Cucinelli is an apparel house closely associated with cashmere and Solomeo. The name identifies a designer and business, not a species, cashmere grade or universal fiber recipe. A collection can include many materials; the composition of each garment remains the starting point.

Textile context

The company connects its beginnings in 1978 with colored cashmere knitwear. Its founder’s account describes experimenting with dyed women’s sweaters before expanding the business. That history concerns product design and commerce, rather than the discovery of cashmere as a fiber.
