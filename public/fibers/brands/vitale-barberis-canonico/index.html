Vitale Barberis Canonico

Vitale Barberis Canonico, often shortened to VBC, is a cloth mill associated with Pratrivero. A fabric carrying its name may be made into a garment by an unrelated tailor or apparel house. The cloth reference is therefore distinct from the identity of the finished garment maker.

Textile context

The mill’s chronology cites a 1663 document for family textile activity and dates the named Vitale Barberis Canonico company to 1936. It also records a complete-cycle factory in Pratrivero in 1910. These are different milestones, not interchangeable claims about the age of one unchanged legal entity.
