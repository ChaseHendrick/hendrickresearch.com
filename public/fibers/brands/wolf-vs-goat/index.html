Wolf vs Goat

An apparel brand that publishes material education about merino micron, mill yarns and cloth construction. It is a reference brand for learning materials language, not a proprietary fiber.

Textile profile · Snapshot 2026-09-27

Read the full textile profile

Textile context

Wolf vs Goat grew as a garment maker that names mill yarns and fiber grades in public product stories. Use those stories to practice separating mill yarn brands from fiber classes; they are attributed examples, not endorsements or proof of every batch.

Company sources describe the producer’s own history and products. They do not independently verify environmental or performance claims. An absent end date does not establish that a business is still trading. Country and headquarters do not establish manufacturing or fiber origin.

Underlying material: Merino wool

The atlas profile for Wolf vs Goat links it to Merino wool as its underlying material. A brand or product name does not change the generic fiber named on a label.
