Yves Salomon

A French house associated with fur, shearling and fine outerwear, including cashmere pieces. It is a reference brand for specialty outerwear materials language, not a proprietary fiber.

Textile context

Yves Salomon’s public story centers on furrier craft and luxury outerwear constructions. Use it to separate house name, animal-fiber classes (for example cashmere or shearling) and finishing; verify current product composition on producer pages. Inclusion teaches materials vocabulary and is not an endorsement of fur.
