Reda

Reda is a wool-textile mill associated with the Biella area. Its range includes cloth collections with distinct names and specifications. Reda, Reda Active and Reda Flexo should be read as company or product-range identities, not as interchangeable generic names for a single fiber.

Textile context

The Biella archive network dates the wool mill founded by Carlo Reda in Valle Mosso to 1865. Reda’s own heritage account describes later manufacturing developments and acquisitions. The year in its branding is a heritage reference, not a fabric weight, wool grade or current production volume.

Company sources describe the producer’s own history and products. They do not independently verify environmental or performance claims. An absent end date does not establish that a business is still trading. Country and headquarters do not establish manufacturing or fiber origin.
