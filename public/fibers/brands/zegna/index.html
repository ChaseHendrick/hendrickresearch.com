ZEGNA

ZEGNA is a menswear brand connected to a textile manufacturing group. Its history begins with a wool mill in Trivero. A Zegna cloth reference and a ZEGNA finished garment describe different products, even when they share a commercial name.

Textile context

The group dates its foundation in Trivero to 1910. Its history describes a progression from wool-textile manufacture to a broader apparel and manufacturing business. The mill, garment brand and corporate group should remain distinct when interpreting product descriptions.
