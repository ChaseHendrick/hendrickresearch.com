Textile context

The brand connects nylon recovery with new yarn and product applications. It is a branded route within polyamide recycling, not a new natural fiber.

Company sources describe the producer’s own history and products. They do not independently verify environmental or performance claims. An absent end date does not establish that a business is still trading. Country and headquarters do not establish manufacturing or fiber origin.
