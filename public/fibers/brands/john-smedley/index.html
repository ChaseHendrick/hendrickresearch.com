John Smedley

John Smedley is a knitwear maker associated with Lea Mills in Derbyshire. Its producer material descriptions distinguish extra-fine merino and the house’s Sea Island Cotton range. These are separate fiber stories; the maker’s name alone does not identify the composition of a particular sweater.

Textile context

The producer history traces Lea Mills to 1784, initially making muslin and spinning cotton. The later knitwear story is a change in manufacturing activity. An old mill date should therefore be read as industrial heritage, not proof that every present product has been made unchanged since that year.
