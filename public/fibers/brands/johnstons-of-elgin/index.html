Johnstons of Elgin

Johnstons of Elgin is a Scottish textile house with weaving and knitwear traditions. Its history distinguishes the Elgin weaving mill and the Hawick knitting operation. Scottish manufacture describes a processing location; it does not establish that cashmere goats or wool-producing sheep were raised in Scotland.

Textile context

The house traces its history to 1797. Its timeline dates the Hawick mill to 1980 and describes that development as a move into knitwear manufacturing. Keeping those dates separate explains why one company name can cover both woven and knitted textile traditions.
