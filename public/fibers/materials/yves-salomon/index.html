Overview

A French house associated with fur, shearling and fine outerwear, including cashmere pieces. It is a reference brand for specialty outerwear materials language, not a proprietary fiber.

Feel and behavior

Depends on the specified fiber grade, yarn and fabric construction.

Source and geography

Yves Salomon is a French house associated with fur, shearling and luxury outerwear, not a fiber class. User shorthand Yves Solomon maps to producer spelling Salomon. Fiber or fur still comes from animal sources specified per product rather than from the house name. This atlas treats the trademark as educational vocabulary for reading hangtags, mill cloth names and generic fiber classes without certifying supply chains, performance claims or animal welfare.

Frequently asked

Did you mean Yves Solomon?

Producer spelling is Yves Salomon. The atlas uses Salomon and treats Solomon as a common misspelling of the same house.

Is this a cashmere fiber brand?

No. It is an outerwear house. Some products use cashmere; many use shearling, fur or other cloths listed on the ticket.

Why include a furrier house?

To teach materials vocabulary around outerwear: house name, animal-fiber class and finishing must be read as separate layers.
