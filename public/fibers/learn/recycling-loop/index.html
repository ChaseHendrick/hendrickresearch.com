Recycled from what? Destined for what?

The two questions behind a recycled-content claim.

Reviewed 2026-09-22. Open in the interactive atlas.

On this page

  1. Identify the input
  2. Distinguish recovery methods
  3. Read the percentage
  4. Plan for the next owner

Identify the input

Factory offcuts and worn garments are different waste streams. Bottle-derived polyester is another. Recycled content describes an input to the current product; it does not automatically establish a route for its next life.

Distinguish recovery methods

Mechanical opening can shorten textile fibers. Polymer recycling follows a different path and has its own feedstock constraints. Color, contamination, blends and finishes affect both approaches.

Read the percentage

A product may contain both recovered and virgin material. Look for a composition statement, the recycled fraction and evidence that applies to the specific product.

Plan for the next owner

Repair, resale and continued use can keep a useful textile in service. A take-back program is meaningful when its actual sorting and downstream outlets are explained.
