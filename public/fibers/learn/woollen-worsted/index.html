HOW IT IS MADE · 3 MIN READ

Two ways to turn wool into a world of cloth.

Woollen and worsted describe processing systems, not sheep breeds.

Reviewed 2026-09-22. Open in the interactive atlas.

Follow the preparation

Cleaned wool is opened and organized before yarn formation. In a worsted route, combing removes shorter fibers and helps align the longer ones. Woollen preparation retains a less parallel arrangement.

Read the surface

Worsted yarns often make smooth, defined tailoring cloth. Woollen yarns can give a fuller, more textured fabric. Finishing can blur that distinction, so these are tendencies rather than promises.

Put the vocabulary to work

When evaluating a sweater or jacket, ask about yarn preparation and construction as well as fiber content. A fine fiber does not automatically produce a durable or comfortable garment.
